# Awesome-Revenue-Management-For-Hotels

## Top Revenue Management for Hotels Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Hotel Revenue Management Systems (RMS), Dynamic Pricing, Demand Forecasting, Open Pricing, Rate Optimization & RevPAR Growth*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Hotel Revenue Management**. These systems help hotels and hotel groups optimize room rates, forecast demand, apply dynamic and open pricing, manage inventory across channels, and maximize RevPAR, ADR, and occupancy.



**Examples** include IDeaS, Duetto (including GameChanger), Atomize, Pace Revenue, RoomPriceGenie, BEONx, Lighthouse, RateGain, and RMS Cloud (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Enterprise-grade hotel RMS platforms with sophisticated forecasting, real-time open pricing, and deep PMS/CRS integrations are almost entirely commercial. Open-source activity is limited to research prototypes, AI-driven dynamic pricing experiments, and basic hotel management systems that include simple pricing logic. This section lists every significant relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[IDeaS](https://ideas.com/)**  

  Long-established enterprise hotel revenue management system (G3 and related solutions) widely used by large hotels and chains for advanced forecasting, pricing, and revenue optimization.



- **[Duetto / GameChanger by Duetto](https://www.duetto.com/)**  

  Cloud-native revenue management platform known for Open Pricing—independent real-time pricing by room type, channel, and segment—popular with mid-size to large hotels and groups.



- **[Atomize](https://www.atomize.com/)**  

  AI-driven RMS focused on fully autonomous, real-time price optimization and demand forecasting, well-suited to independent and mid-size hotels seeking high automation.



- **[RoomPriceGenie](https://www.roompricegenie.com/)**  

  User-friendly, highly automated revenue management system frequently ranked top for independent hotels and properties without a dedicated revenue manager.



- **[BEONx, Pace Revenue, Lighthouse](https://www.beonx.com/)**  

  Specialized RMS and pricing intelligence platforms serving various hotel segments with forecasting, competitive rate shopping, and optimization tools.



- **[RateGain](https://rategain.com/)**  

  Hospitality technology provider offering revenue management, rate intelligence, and distribution solutions for hotels and travel companies.



- **[RMS Cloud](https://www.rmscloud.com/)**  

  Property and revenue management oriented platform with tools for rate control, distribution, and hotel operations.



- **[Other hotel RMS & pricing platforms](https://ideas.com/)**  

  Additional commercial solutions covering group pricing, casino/hotel hybrid revenue, and multi-property optimization.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Data & forecasting stacks**: Prophet, statsmodels, or modern ML libraries used to build custom demand forecasts from PMS data.

- **Competitive rate shopping helpers**: Scripts that scrape or ingest public rates (with legal/ToS caution) for benchmarking.

- **PMS integration connectors**: Open APIs and middleware that push recommended rates into channel managers or booking engines.

- **Dashboarding**: Metabase, Superset, or Grafana for visualizing occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, and pricing performance.

- **Workflow automation**: n8n or similar tools to orchestrate rate updates and alerts.

- Spreadsheet-to-model bridges still common among smaller properties for simple rule-based pricing.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

True enterprise hotel revenue management (accurate demand forecasting, real-time open pricing, multi-segment controls, and deep PMS integrations) remains the domain of commercial platforms (IDeaS, Duetto, Atomize, RoomPriceGenie, etc.).  

Open-source efforts are strongest as research prototypes, AI pricing engines, and educational tools. Smaller properties sometimes combine an open-source or lightweight PMS with custom pricing scripts or simple rule engines.  

Most hotels that need reliable, automated revenue optimization adopt a commercial RMS while using open-source components for supplementary analytics or experimentation.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Hotel revenue management systems directly affect pricing, inventory, and financial performance. Incorrect models or poor data quality can lead to significant revenue loss or guest dissatisfaction.

- Open-source pricing tools and prototypes require careful validation, integration with live PMS/channel data, and ongoing monitoring. They are not substitutes for proven commercial RMS platforms in production hotel environments. Operators remain responsible for pricing decisions and regulatory compliance (including consumer pricing rules).
